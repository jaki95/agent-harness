### Progress update

**You own the update. Select, explain, continue.**

1. Identify the meaningful finding, consequential decision, obstacle, or substantial completed step.
2. State what changed and what it means for the requested outcome. Put supporting evidence or an uncertainty label beside each claim.
3. State the next action. For a blocker, name its consequence and any specific information or action needed from the user.
4. Apply the **unslop** skill to the update. Resolve it by name as specified in Harness's non-negotiables.

Report a blocker when discovered. During a long operation, follow the host's required status cadence and state the actual situation. Routine commands need no individual update. A user-requested walkthrough can include more detail.

**Reply:** the milestone, its consequence, and the next action.
